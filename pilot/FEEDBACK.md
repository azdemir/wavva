# Pilot Feedback Form (mid-pilot + exit)

Ask on a call — don't email a survey. Record answers verbatim.

## Usage (mid-pilot, week 4)

1. Walk me through your last booking, start to finish. Where did you get stuck?
2. How many quotes have you sent from TidyPro? How long does a quote take vs. before?
3. Have your cleaners used **My jobs** on their phones? What did they say?
4. Have any customers used the portal link (approve quote / pay invoice)?
5. What's the one thing you'd change first?

## Value (exit, week 6)

6. Roughly how many hours per week did TidyPro save you? What did you do instead?
7. Did you get paid faster on any invoice? (portal vs. before)
8. What would make you cancel after the pilot?
9. If we charged $49/month starting tomorrow, would you pay? What about $99?
10. Would you introduce us to one other cleaning company owner? (Ask only if
    answers to 6–9 are positive.)

## Scoring notes for us

- "Hours saved" is the headline metric — push for a number, not "a lot".
- A "yes" to Q9 at $49 from 2 of 3 companies = green light for paid launch.
- Every complaint is a roadmap item; log them with the company name and date.
