# TidyPro Pilot Agreement (template)

> This is a starting template, not legal advice. Have a local attorney review
> before using it with real companies.

**Between:** TidyPro ("we") and ______________________ ("Company").

**Pilot period:** ______ to ______ (recommended: 60 days).

**What you get**
- Free access to TidyPro for up to ___ users during the pilot.
- Setup help: a 30-minute onboarding call and data entry assistance.
- Direct support by phone/text during business hours.

**What we ask**
- Use TidyPro for real quotes, bookings, and invoices during the pilot.
- A 15-minute feedback call each week (4–6 calls total).
- Permission to fix bugs you report and to anonymize usage learnings.

**Your data**
- Your customers, schedules, and financial data are isolated to your
  workspace and never shared with other companies.
- You can export your data at any time; after the pilot we delete it on
  request within 30 days.

**Payments**
- No charges during the pilot. If you continue after, paid plans start at
  $49/month (final pricing confirmed at launch).
- Customer card payments during the pilot run in Stripe test mode unless we
  agree otherwise in writing.

**No warranties.** The pilot software is provided as-is for evaluation.

Signed: ______________________________ Date: __________
