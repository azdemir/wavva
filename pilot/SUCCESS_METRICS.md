# Pilot Success Metrics

Evaluate at the end of week 6. Green-light paid launch only if most of these hold.

## Activation (per company)

- [ ] Onboarding wizard completed without our help on the call
- [ ] ≥ 5 real customers and ≥ 3 real services entered
- [ ] ≥ 1 cleaner invited and opening **My jobs** on their phone

## Real usage (per company, weeks 3–6)

- [ ] ≥ 5 quotes created, ≥ 2 approved (ideally via the portal link)
- [ ] ≥ 5 bookings created, ≥ 5 job visits completed in the app
- [ ] ≥ 2 invoices sent, ≥ 1 payment recorded

## Value signals

- [ ] Owner reports ≥ 2 hours/week saved on scheduling/quoting/invoicing
- [ ] Zero data-loss or wrong-tenant incidents (non-negotiable)
- [ ] 2 of 3 companies say yes to $49/month (see `FEEDBACK.md` Q9)

## Red lines (any one = stop and fix)

- Any customer data visible to the wrong company
- A lost booking or invoice (data integrity failure)
- Owner can't complete the core loop without calling us twice for the same thing

## After green light

1. Fix `SECURITY.md` P0 items (role tightening, rate limits, backups)
2. Confirm final pricing and Stripe Billing for platform subscriptions
3. Convert pilots to paid; ask for the referral (feedback Q10)
